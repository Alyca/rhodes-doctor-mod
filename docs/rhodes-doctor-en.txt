ARCHETYPE
══════════════════════════════════════════

Name: Rhodes Island Doctor
Class: Paladin

[Character creation description]
The Doctor of Rhodes Island awoke from the sarcophagus with nearly all memories lost — yet with
a mind that still reads the battlefield like an open book. Rarely raising a blade in person, the
Doctor directs every ally with brief, precise orders. But beneath that calm exterior slumbers
the one once called the Specter of Babel. When the battle demands it, the Doctor lets it wake
for a short while, turning every shred of wisdom into the sharpest fighting instinct.

[Changes to the paladin class]
Class skills: adds Knowledge (Arcana), Knowledge (World), and Perception to the paladin's class
skills.
Skill ranks per level: 4 + Intelligence modifier.
Hit die, base attack bonus, saving throws, proficiencies, alignment, and code of conduct:
unchanged.


══════════════════════════════════════════
CLASS FEATURES
══════════════════════════════════════════

Tactical Orders (Sp)
At 1st level, the Doctor gains the Tactical Orders spellbook: a standalone arcane spellbook of
supernatural orders. It is a spontaneous spellbook cast with Intelligence; spells per day and
spells known exactly follow the sorcerer table. The Doctor casts any known order freely while
slots of the matching level remain — no preparation is required.
New spell levels are gained at paladin levels 1, 4, 6, 8, 10, 12, 14, 16, and 18 (levels 1–9).
0-level orders are cantrips: usable at will, unlimited times per day. Starting at 4th level, at
every even level the Doctor may forget one known order and learn a new one of the same or a
lower level; cantrips cannot be swapped.
This spellbook ignores arcane spell failure from armor. Unless an order says otherwise, orders
are subject to dispelling, antimagic, spell resistance, and counterspelling; their save DC
equals 10 + order level + the Doctor's Intelligence modifier, and their caster level equals the
Doctor's paladin level.
This ability, together with Focus Fire, replaces smite evil.

──────────────────────────────

Focus Fire (Ex)
At 1st level, the Doctor can use Focus Fire 3 + Intelligence modifier times per day (minimum 1).
It is not a spell and costs no spell slot.
Swift action; range 60 ft.; target one enemy; duration: Intelligence modifier rounds (min 1);
Saving Throw: none.
The Doctor marks the target. All allies gain a +1 insight bonus on attack rolls and damage
rolls against it, increasing by +1 for every 4 paladin levels (to +6 at 20th). Only one mark
can be active at a time; marking a new target ends the old one.

──────────────────────────────

Originium Arts Mastery (Ex)
At 1st level, the Doctor gains a +2 bonus on caster level checks to overcome spell resistance.
This bonus increases to +4 at 7th level, +6 at 13th, and +8 at 19th.
While the Specter of Babel is active, this bonus is lost and the Doctor instead takes a penalty
on such checks equal to the Transfer Value. After gaining Doctor of Rhodes Island at 20th
level, this penalty no longer applies.
This ability replaces divine grace.

──────────────────────────────

Cool Head (Ex)
At 2nd level, the Doctor adds their Intelligence modifier (if positive) as an untyped bonus on
all saving throws. While the Specter of Babel is active, this bonus is halved (rounded down);
at 20th level it is no longer halved.

──────────────────────────────

Lay on Hands (Su)
As the paladin ability, except that all values based on Charisma use Intelligence instead: uses
per day equal half the Doctor's paladin level + Intelligence modifier, and the DC when harming
undead uses the Intelligence modifier.
This ability modifies lay on hands.

──────────────────────────────

Channel Positive Energy (Su)
As the paladin ability, except that the DC equals 10 + half the Doctor's paladin level + the
Doctor's Intelligence modifier.
This ability modifies channel positive energy.

──────────────────────────────

Aura of Command (Ex)
At 4th level, the Doctor emits a 10-foot aura. The Doctor and allies within gain a +1
competence bonus on attack rolls and initiative checks. Suppressed while the Doctor is
unconscious or the Specter of Babel is active.
This ability replaces the paladin's original spellcasting.

──────────────────────────────

Total Command (Ex)
At 11th level, Aura of Command's radius increases to 30 feet and its bonus to +2.
This ability replaces aura of justice.

──────────────────────────────

Specter of Babel (Ex)
At 8th level, the Doctor can awaken the other self slumbering within. Activation is a move
action (a swift action from 16th level); ending it is a free action. The Specter can be
maintained for 4 + paladin level + Intelligence modifier rounds per day, in any number of
bouts, restored after rest. The Transfer Value equals half the Intelligence modifier at levels
8–11, and the full Intelligence modifier from 12th level. The Doctor's Intelligence score
itself is never reduced.

On activation, choose one form; it cannot be changed until the Specter ends:
- Agile Form: an untyped bonus to Dexterity equal to twice the Transfer Value; melee attack and
  damage rolls use Dexterity instead of Strength (whichever is higher); the armor's maximum
  Dexterity bonus increases by the Transfer Value. From 12th level, evasion that works in any
  armor.
- Enduring Form: an untyped bonus to Constitution equal to twice the Transfer Value (hit points
  and Fortitude saves rise accordingly). From 12th level, damage reduction 3/— that stacks with
  damage reduction from other sources.

While the Specter is active:
- the Doctor can only cast orders with the [Specter] descriptor (Focus Fire is allowed from
  16th level);
- Originium Arts Mastery is lost and its caster-level checks take a penalty equal to the
  Transfer Value;
- Cool Head is halved; Aura of Command and Total Command are suppressed.
Orders already in effect last out their remaining duration.

When the Specter ends, for any reason, the Doctor suffers the Specter's Aftermath for 2 rounds:
only move actions are allowed (no attacks, spells, or active abilities or items), and speed
gains a +50-foot untyped bonus. The Aftermath cannot be ended early and the Specter cannot be
reactivated during it.
This ability replaces aura of resolve.

──────────────────────────────

Doctor of Rhodes Island (Ex)
At 20th level, while the Specter is active the Doctor may cast all orders, Cool Head and the
auras function at full strength, and Originium Arts Mastery no longer suffers its penalty. When
the Specter ends, the Aftermath imposes no action restriction and the Specter can be
reactivated immediately; the +50-foot speed still applies for 2 rounds.
This ability replaces holy champion.

0-LEVEL ORDERS (CANTRIPS)
══════════════════════════════════════════

Cantrips are arcane spells with a casting time of 1 standard action; spell resistance: no,
unless noted.

Tactical Mark — Divination; range 60 ft.; one creature; 1 round; no save.
You and your allies gain a +1 insight bonus on the next attack roll against the target this
round. A creature can be affected only once per round.

Field Dressing — Transmutation (healing); touch; one living creature; instantaneous; Will
(harmless).
The target regains 1 hit point. Cannot affect a creature that has benefited from it in the last
24 hours.

Battlefield Survey — Divination; personal; you; 1 minute.
+2 insight bonus on Perception checks.

Beacon Projection — Illusion (light); range 30 ft.; one floating beacon; 10 min./level; no
save.
Creates a beacon illuminating a 20-ft. radius.

Suppressing Shot — Enchantment (mind-affecting); range 30 ft.; one enemy; 1 round/level; Will
negates.
The target takes a –2 penalty on its next attack roll.

Emergency Extraction — Conjuration (teleportation); touch; you or one willing ally;
instantaneous; Will (harmless).
The target moves up to 10 ft. to a visible square (implemented as the nearest open square
behind the caster). This movement provokes no attacks of opportunity.

IMPLEMENTATION NOTES
══════════════════════════════════════════

1. A standalone spontaneous arcane spellbook: sorcerer slots/known table, Intelligence-based,
   no arcane spell failure from armor.
2. All non-cantrip orders are BlueprintAbility spells added to this class's spell list;
   cantrips are unlimited-use 0-level spells.
3. Focus Fire remains a resource-based class feature (3 + Intelligence modifier per day, min 1).
4. While the Specter is active, a caster restriction allows only spells with the [Specter]
   descriptor; at 20th level the restriction is removed. Originium Arts Mastery's SR-check
   bonus inverts to a penalty of the Transfer Value during the Specter; no penalty at 20th.
